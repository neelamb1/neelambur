People searching for used-item marketplaces around Coimbatore are usually trying to buy or sell something nearby with less delivery cost and delay. Local exchange can be useful, but a nearby location or familiar phone number does not prove that an item, buyer, seller, or payment request is genuine.

This checklist complements the article on writing a good classified ad. It focuses on the transaction itself.

## Before agreeing to buy

Ask for the exact item description, model, age, condition, included parts, known faults, ownership context, and current photos. For an expensive or regulated item, check the documents needed to transfer ownership lawfully.

Compare the description with the item during inspection. Test normal functions in a safe setting and ask whether repairs, account locks, finance, warranty, or missing accessories affect use. A serial number can help identify an item, but do not publish sensitive identifiers unnecessarily.

Do not send an advance merely because the seller says other buyers are waiting. If delivery is required, use a payment and shipping method whose terms you understand.

## Before agreeing to sell

Remove personal data and signed-in accounts from phones, computers, storage devices, and smart equipment. Back up what you need, follow the manufacturer's reset process, and remove SIM or memory cards.

State the condition honestly and keep the final price and included items in writing. Do not accept a screenshot as proof of payment. Check the actual bank or payment-app status through your own account.

## Meet and pay safely

Use a public, appropriate meeting place during practical hours when possible. Tell a trusted person about the meeting, avoid carrying unnecessary cash, and do not invite an unverified stranger into a private home merely to complete a sale.

To receive money through UPI, a seller does not need to enter a UPI PIN to approve a collect request. Never share an OTP, PIN, card code, or remote-screen access. If something appears fraudulent, stop the transaction and use the official cybercrime reporting channel.

## Close the listing

Mark the item sold and remove public personal details that are no longer needed. Keep transaction records only as long as there is a legitimate reason and handle personal data carefully.
