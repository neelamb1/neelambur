A local directory becomes trustworthy when wrong or unsafe information can be corrected. Reports are not only for serious abuse. They also help fix ordinary problems before people waste time or get misled.

For Neelambur.com, reporting should be simple, specific, and useful for review.

## What is worth reporting

Useful reports include:

- Wrong phone number or contact path.
- Moved, closed, or duplicate listing.
- Wrong category or unclear service description.
- Fake ownership claim.
- Suspicious payment request or misleading contact detail.
- Unsafe listing content.
- Harassment, impersonation, spam, or repeated misuse.

Reports should focus on facts that can be checked. Personal fights, rumours, and vague complaints are difficult to use and should not be published as claims.

## What to include in a report

A good report should explain what is wrong and how it can be checked. For example, say which field is wrong, what the current correct detail is if you know it, and whether you are the owner, customer, nearby resident, or someone who noticed the issue.

Screenshots or public proof can help when available, but private details should be avoided unless they are necessary for review.

## What happens after a report

Not every report should immediately remove a listing. Some reports need a simple correction. Some need owner confirmation. Some need the listing to be hidden until the risk is reviewed.

Important changes should leave an audit trail. A directory that changes public information should be able to explain what changed, when it changed, and why it changed.

## Reporting helps everyone

Accurate local information saves time. It helps users find the right place, helps owners receive the right calls, and helps the site avoid becoming a pile of outdated entries.

If you see wrong or unsafe information, report the exact issue. Clear reports make the directory more useful for the next person.
