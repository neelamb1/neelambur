If a useful local place is missing from Neelambur.com, the best help is a clear suggestion with enough detail for review. A short, accurate note is better than a long message with uncertain claims.

The directory should grow through verified local input. That means missing places can be suggested, but they should be checked before appearing publicly.

## What to send

When suggesting a missing place, include only details you are comfortable having reviewed:

- Place or business name.
- Category, such as food, daily needs, repair, education, healthcare, transport, service, temple, institution, or rental help.
- Location context, such as nearby landmark, road, area, or building.
- Public contact path if it is already meant for customers or visitors.
- What the place is useful for.
- Whether you are the owner, a customer, a nearby resident, or someone sharing a correction.

Do not send private phone numbers, private addresses, personal disputes, or allegations that cannot be checked.

## What makes a suggestion easier to review

A reviewer should be able to understand the place without guessing. For example, "small electrical repair shop near the main road" is more useful than "good shop". "Open mostly in the evening" is useful only when you know it is true.

If you are unsure, say that you are unsure. Uncertain details can still help, but they should not be presented as verified.

## Why review matters

Local directories can become messy if every suggestion goes live immediately. Wrong contact details, duplicate entries, moved locations, and fake ownership claims reduce trust quickly.

That is why Neelambur.com should review missing-place suggestions before publication. The goal is not to slow down helpful input. The goal is to keep the directory useful after it grows.

## Keep suggestions local and practical

Suggest places that help people around Neelambur solve real needs. Shops, services, institutions, useful public places, local contacts, and recurring community needs are good candidates.

If you know a missing place that would help others, send the clearest details you have. The directory becomes better when local knowledge is shared carefully.
