A local job post can connect nearby employers and workers quickly, but only when the information is clear enough to verify. Messages such as "staff wanted, call immediately" leave applicants guessing about the employer, work, location, pay, and legitimacy of the contact.

A useful opening should describe the role without asking applicants to take unnecessary risks.

## Include the basic job details

A clear local job post should include:

- Employer or business name.
- Job title and main responsibilities.
- General work location.
- Full-time, part-time, temporary, shift, contract, or apprenticeship status.
- Expected working hours or shift pattern.
- Pay amount or a realistic pay range when available.
- Required skills, experience, licence, or language ability.
- Public application contact or verified application link.
- Application deadline or whether hiring remains open.

State whether the contact is the employer, an authorised recruiter, or someone forwarding the opening. Do not present an unverified forwarded message as a confirmed vacancy.

## Do not collect unnecessary information

An initial application may need a name, contact path, relevant experience, and résumé. It should not require a bank PIN, one-time password, card detail, or payment to unlock an interview.

Identity and banking information may be needed later for lawful hiring and payroll, but it should be collected by the verified employer through an appropriate process-not through an unexplained public form or casual message.

## Warning signs applicants should check

Be cautious when a job promises unusually high pay for unclear work, guarantees selection without an interview, demands money for registration or equipment, or pressures the applicant to act immediately. Verify the employer using an independent public contact path instead of relying only on the number in a forwarded message.

Before travelling, confirm the interview location, contact person, role, and time. Tell someone where you are going when meeting an unfamiliar employer or recruiter.

## Close filled openings

Once a role is filled or the deadline passes, mark the post closed. Old job posts create repeated calls for employers and false hope for applicants.

If a job post contains a false employer name, payment demand, impersonation, or misleading role description, report the exact listing and evidence available for review.
